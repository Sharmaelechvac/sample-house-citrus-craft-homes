Rough-In & Inspection

Boxes and wire done neat, photographed, then inspected.

  • Boxes set solid and at the same height; cable stapled flat; kick plates where wire passes studs.
  • Our crew photographs each room before the walls close. You see them the same day.
  • Inspection date is auto-checked with the county and shows on the house page.

Trim-out: devices

After paint, devices and plates go in.

  • TR (tamper-resistant) receptacles inside.
  • WR receptacles with in-use covers outside.
  • GFCI and AFCI tested.
